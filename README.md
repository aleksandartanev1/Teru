# Teru
Teru is an automated daily scheduler that maps your fixed commitments (work, classes, events) and dynamically generates and time-blocks daily gym workouts and balanced meal schedules into your free time.

During my everyday life I discovered google calendar that fixed some of my problems with remembering things. But it didn't quite fix everything. I still had to figure out on what days I workout, on what days I workout which muscles, what do I eat everyday, when do I eat it, when's the optimal time to do what and etc. So this project aims to alleviate some of the troubles I have with scheduling and hopefully develop into something better.
